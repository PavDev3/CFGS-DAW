TIEMPOS VERBALES - INGLÉS
(cuándo usar cada uno)

══ PRESENTE ══

PRESENT SIMPLE
I work / he works
Usar para:
• Hábitos y rutinas
• Hechos y verdades
• Horarios fijos
Señales: always, usually,
often, never, every day
Neg: don't / doesn't work
Preg: Do you work?
He/she/it + S

PRESENT CONTINUOUS
I am working
Usar para:
• Ahora mismo
• Algo temporal
• Planes futuros cerca
• Quejas (always + -ing)
Señales: now, at the moment,
today, this week
Neg: I'm not working
Preg: Are you working?

PRESENT PERFECT
I have worked
Usar para:
• Experiencia de vida
• Pasado con efecto hoy
• Acción sin tiempo concreto
• Algo que empezó y sigue
Señales: ever, never, already,
yet, just, since, for, so far
Neg: haven't / hasn't worked
OJO: NO con fecha concreta
(yesterday, in 2020)

PRESENT PERFECT CONT.
I have been working
Usar para:
• Acción que empezó y sigue
• Énfasis en la duración
• Resultado visible reciente
Señales: for, since, all day,
lately, recently
"I've been waiting for 2 hours"

══ PASADO ══

PAST SIMPLE
I worked
Usar para:
• Acción terminada
• Momento concreto del pasado
• Secuencia de acciones
Señales: yesterday, last week,
ago, in 2020, when
Neg: didn't work
Preg: Did you work?
Irregulares: go-went, see-saw

PAST CONTINUOUS
I was working
Usar para:
• Acción en progreso en el
  pasado
• Acción interrumpida
• Dos acciones a la vez
Señales: while, when, as
"I was sleeping when you
called"
(larga = continuous,
corta = simple)

PAST PERFECT
I had worked
Usar para:
• Acción anterior a otra
  acción pasada
• "Lo que pasó antes"
Señales: before, after,
by the time, already, when
"When I arrived, he had left"

PAST PERFECT CONT.
I had been working
Usar para:
• Duración de algo anterior
  a otro momento pasado
"I had been waiting for an
hour when she arrived"

══ FUTURO ══

WILL
I will work
Usar para:
• Decisión en el momento
• Predicción (opinión)
• Promesas y ofertas
• Hechos futuros
Señales: probably, I think,
I'm sure, maybe
Neg: won't work

BE GOING TO
I am going to work
Usar para:
• Planes e intenciones ya
  decididos
• Predicción con evidencia
  presente
"Look at those clouds! It's
going to rain"

FUTURE CONTINUOUS
I will be working
Usar para:
• Acción en progreso en un
  momento futuro
"This time tomorrow I'll be
flying to Madrid"

FUTURE PERFECT
I will have worked
Usar para:
• Acción terminada antes de
  un momento futuro
Señales: by + fecha,
by the time
"By 2030 I will have
finished my degree"

FUTURE PERFECT CONT.
I will have been working
Usar para:
• Duración hasta un momento
  futuro
"In June I'll have been
working here for 5 years"

══ OTROS FUTUROS ══

Present Continuous (futuro)
Plan CON fecha/hora acordada
"I'm meeting Ana at 5"

Present Simple (futuro)
Horarios y calendarios
"The train leaves at 9"

══ TRUCOS RÁPIDOS ══

• Fecha concreta → Past Simple
• "Ever/never/yet" → Pres. Perfect
• "Since + punto" (since 2020)
• "For + duración" (for 3 years)
• Reloj/horario → Pres. Simple
• Plan decidido → going to
• Decisión ahora → will
• Dos pasados: el 1º → Past
  Perfect, el 2º → Past Simple
• Después de if/when/while/
  before/after/as soon as NO
  se usa will:
  "When I arrive, I'll call"

══ CONDICIONALES ══

0: If + pres, pres
  (hechos) If you heat ice,
  it melts
1: If + pres, will
  (posible) If it rains,
  I'll stay
2: If + past, would
  (improbable) If I won, I'd
  travel
3: If + past perfect,
  would have + pp
  (imposible, pasado)
  If I had studied, I would
  have passed
